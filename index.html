The Proactive.

Simulating a hug when you need it.

Developed by Vayence Inc, ProActive is a reusable, self-inflating vest for people with autism and sensory sensitivities.

When its sensors pick up rising stress, the vest inflates to apply deep pressure, then deflates once the wearer is calm.

The problem

Sensory overload happens in everyday places. Classrooms, grocery stores, transit and waiting rooms can all trigger it. These moments are hard to predict, and most existing products are either ineffective, too intrusive, or can’t respond when overload actually happens.

Sensory overwhelm.
It can happen anywhere.

Everyday triggers

  • Sound Loud or sudden noise.
  • Light Bright or flickering lights.
  • Crowds Busy, crowded spaces.
  • Touch Uncomfortable fabrics and unexpected contact.
How it works

How ProActive works

The vest monitors the wearer throughout the day and only applies pressure when it’s needed.

Step 01

Monitor

Sensors in contact with the skin track biometric signals linked to rising stress.

Step 02

Relief

When stress rises, a built-in compressor fills air bladders inside the vest, applying firm, even pressure similar to a hug.

Step 03

Tracking

The vest stays inflated until readings return to baseline or the wearer turns it off, then deflates gradually.
