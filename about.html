About Vayence Inc

Our mission is to help people navigate their environments with confidence, using technology and expertise.

Vayence Inc is a Toronto-based health and medical technology company founded by industrial designers Marzuq Vohra and Vienna Doria-Pighin. ProActive, a deep pressure vest for autistic people and others with sensory sensitivities, is its first product.
